DEW SCAPES

Website concept · October 2026

A website that grows.

A new landing page for Dew Scapes, built around one idea: as visitors scroll, a garden grows in front of them.

The idea

Visitors don't read about growth. They watch it.

One continuous film runs behind the page. Scrolling down grows a seed into a mature shade tree; scrolling up plays it back. Each stage of growth carries one message.

Dew Scapes02

The page, stage by stage

Four messages, one scroll.

  • Seed. “Every garden begins small.” and the first call to book a visit.
  • Sprout. The four services: design, planting, irrigation, care.
  • Sapling. The four-step process, from site visit to care plan.
  • Shade tree. The payoff, and the invitation to book.
Dew Scapes03

Where every visit ends

One clear action: book a site visit.

A short form asks for name, mobile, district, garden size and what they need. Once connected, each request lands directly in the Dew sales pipeline, the same platform we showed in the meeting.

Arabic and English, phone first

Made for how your clients actually browse.

The whole page switches to Arabic with one tap and reads right to left. On a phone the film fills the screen and the text stacks cleanly.
